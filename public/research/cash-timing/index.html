    Calculation Rules Used in This Demo
    
        
      
          
    • An unpaid bill remains reserved when its due date arrives or passes.
      • 
          
    • A cleared bill leaves the pending list only when the displayed posted balance is reduced by the same amount once.
      • 
          
    • An unreceived paycheck is not counted as current funds before its confirmation date.
      • 
          
    • The chosen reserve is applied to the lowest projected balance before the next confirmed paycheck.
      • 
          
    • If the resulting transferable amount is negative, maximum transfer is shown as $0 and the shortfall is shown separately.
